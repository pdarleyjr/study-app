## Religion p.45 — Unit 1 Review "Looking Back at Unit 1" (paraphrase)
Jesus comes to us through the Holy Spirit and helps us give perfect worship to the Father. The Sacraments of
Initiation are Baptism, Confirmation, and the Eucharist; they enable us to work with Christ for the salvation of
the whole world. In Baptism we begin to be Christians and live the Christian life. Through Confirmation we are
strengthened to grow in this life. In the Eucharist, Jesus Christ offers himself for us and nourishes God's life
in us. United with Jesus Christ, we are able to share his love with others.
"Living the Message" checklist: use sacramentals to remember God loves me; praise and thank God daily; tell Jesus
I love him when I receive the Eucharist; give witness to Jesus by good words and kind acts; ask Jesus for help
when tempted. "A Moment with Jesus": a prayer adapted from "Radiating Christ" (St. John Henry Newman).

## Religion p.46 — "About the Sacraments" (acrostic down the word SACRAMENTS) — answers

A — what the bishop places over those to be confirmed as he anoints them → HAND
C — a person in the OCIA who desires to become a Catholic → CATECHUMEN
R — the holy oil used in Confirmation → CHRISM
A — what we do for one hour before receiving the Eucharist → FAST
M — sacred vessel used to expose the Blessed Sacrament → MONSTRANCE
E — the day when Jesus gave the Holy Spirit to the Apostles → PENTECOST
N — the sacrament that strengthens the gift of the Holy Spirit first received in Baptism → CONFIRMATION
T — the sacrament in which we receive our first call to give witness to Jesus → BAPTISM
S — the person the Holy Spirit helps us imitate → CHRIST
