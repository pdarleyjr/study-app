# Wild Robot Quest — source (family-supplied chapter review)

Book: *The Wild Robot Escapes* by Peter Brown. Chapters 38–39, pages 103–107 (the family's summary covers these
pages; do not claim page 108 is covered). This summary is the ONLY source. Do not add events, names, places or
details that are not below. Paraphrase in your own friendly words; do not quote the novel.

## Chapter 38 — The Truth  (memory hook: Chapter 38 = SECRET REVEALED 🔓)
1. Jaya and Jad hear strange noises. They sneak into the barn and find Roz surrounded by cows and geese, holding
   a goose in her arms.
2. They realize the stories Roz told them about a robot living on an island were really stories about Roz herself.
3. Roz admits she lived on an island with wild animals, and that the goose in her arms, Brightbill, is her son
   (her adopted goose son).
4. She kept it secret because she was afraid of frightening the children, and she fears their father will send her
   back to the robot factory to be destroyed if he learns the truth.
5. Jaya and Jad feel compassion for Roz and promise not to tell their father.
Lesson: compassion = caring about other people's feelings and wanting to help them. Instead of being afraid, the
children understand Roz's situation and choose to protect her. Themes: compassion, trust, acceptance, empathy.

## Chapter 39 — The Allies  (memory hook: Chapter 39 = ESCAPE PLANNED 🗺️)
1. The children want Roz to be happy. Because they miss their own mother, they understand how important it is for
   Roz and Brightbill to be together.
2. They tell Roz she belongs on her island with Brightbill, her family and her animal friends.
3. Escaping is dangerous: their father can track Roz's movements. If she tries to escape he might think she is
   defective and send her away to be destroyed.
4. Jaya wonders whether Roz might actually be defective. Roz says she doesn't feel broken, just different.
5. Jaya agrees: being different does not mean something is wrong with you.
6. Jad promises to help — Roz once saved Jad's life, and now he wants to save hers. The children decide to research
   a safe escape plan, because Roz can only access farming information.
7. Brightbill must lead his flock south to their wintering grounds. The children hope to have Roz ready to escape
   when Brightbill returns in the spring.
8. Even though getting back to her island will be difficult, Roz trusts Brightbill and the children to help her.
Lesson: friendship, teamwork and courage help overcome hard problems. Allies = people working together to help and
protect someone. Themes: friendship, courage, teamwork, loyalty, accepting differences.

## Vocabulary from the summary
compassion; allies; defective = not working the way something is supposed to; migrate / wintering grounds (where
the geese fly south for winter); track (follow where someone goes).
